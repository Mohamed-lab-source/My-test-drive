# Content rating and audience answers

**Content rating questionnaire (IARC)** — category: *All other app types*.
- Violence, sex, language, drugs, gambling: **No** to all.
- Does the app let users interact or exchange content? **Yes** (group chats with friends and invite codes).
- Does the app share the user's location with other users? **Yes** (only when the user taps "Send my location" or "Check in").
- Does the app allow purchases? **No**.

**Target audience and content:** choose **18 and over**.
Why: Google's terms for the Gemini AI service restrict using it in apps aimed at people under 18 (check the current terms at ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms before publishing), and 18+ also keeps Khroga out of the extra "Families" rules. You can revisit this later, e.g. 13+ with the AI switched off for younger people.

**Ads:** No, the app doesn't contain ads.
**App access:** All features are available without special access (people can try it as a guest).
**News app / COVID / government / financial features:** No.
